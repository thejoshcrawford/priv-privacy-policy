The essentials

Quick answers, without a manual.

01 / SETUP

Start a vault

Create a passphrase and save your recovery code somewhere safe. You can enable Face ID for faster unlocking.

02 / ADD MEDIA

Keep it in Priv

Capture photos and videos in Priv or import them from Photos. Importing makes a copy; it does not remove the original from your photo library.

03 / SHARE

Pair nearby

Open Priv on both iPhones to pair and sync directly. Each person keeps an encrypted copy on their own device.

Need a hand?

For setup, pairing, purchases, or a problem with your vault, contact the developer. Please mention Priv and describe what happened. Never send your passphrase or recovery code.
