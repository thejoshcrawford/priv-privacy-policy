Privacy Policy

Effective September 26, 2026

Priv is a private photo and video vault. Your vault is stored on your device; we do not operate a cloud vault or upload your media to our servers.

This policy describes the Priv iOS app and this privacy-policy website. “We” refers to the developer of Priv. It does not cover Apple’s own services or the privacy practices of a person with whom you choose to pair your device.

Information the app handles

  • Vault content: photos, videos, thumbnails, associated metadata, and deletion records you create or import are stored locally in encrypted vault files. The app also handles the passphrase, recovery information, and cryptographic material needed to unlock and protect the vault. We do not receive your vault contents, passphrase, or recovery code.
  • Device permissions: with your permission, Priv uses the camera and microphone to capture media, the system photo picker to import selected items, biometrics to unlock if you enable them, and local-network access for nearby pairing, sync, and computer import. Priv does not add captured or imported media to your system Photos library automatically.
  • Nearby sync: if you pair with another device, Priv discovers it nearby and transfers encrypted vault changes directly between devices. A display name and device identity are used for pairing and retained on your device. Content received by the Priv app on the other device is then subject to that app’s storage and deletion choices. We do not relay this sync through our servers.
  • Computer import: when you start this feature, Priv temporarily opens a token-protected HTTP import page on your local network. Anyone with the exact import link who can reach your phone on that network could submit files while it is active. The connection is local-network HTTP, not HTTPS; use a trusted network and close the import screen when finished. Temporary uploads are removed after processing or when the import session ends.
  • Purchases: Apple processes in-app purchases and subscriptions. Priv receives product and verified entitlement information through StoreKit to determine whether unlimited storage is available. We do not receive your payment-card details. Apple’s handling of your account, payment, and transaction information is governed by Apple’s privacy policy.

Priv does not include a third-party analytics or advertising SDK. We do not sell your personal information or use your vault contents for advertising.

How data is stored and shared

Vault files, including originals, thumbnails, and metadata, are encrypted on your device and excluded from iCloud backup by the app. Optional biometric unlock uses iOS and Keychain; Priv does not receive your fingerprint or face data. For video playback and certain import or export operations, the app may create temporary local files, which it attempts to remove when the operation ends. No app can guarantee prevention of screenshots or capture by another camera.

Media moves off your device only when you choose to export or share it, create and transfer a vault migration package, import files from a computer, or sync with a paired device. A migration package is encrypted; protect the package and its passphrase wherever you store or send them. A copy you share or sync may remain with its recipient even after you delete the local copy.

Retention and your choices

Your vault remains on your device until you delete items or remove the app and its data. Deletions create encrypted records used to synchronize deletions with a paired device. You can turn off biometric unlock, unpair a device, stop computer import, delete vault items, or remove the app. Deleting the app does not erase files you previously exported or copies already held by another device. If a subscription ends, existing vault items are not automatically deleted or hidden; the free-tier item limit applies to adding more items. Apple manages purchase records and subscription cancellation in your Apple account.

This website

When you visit this policy page, Cloudflare (our DNS and web proxy) and GitHub Pages (our website host) may process ordinary request information such as your IP address, browser details, request time, and security-related data to serve and protect the page. We do not add analytics or advertising trackers to this page. See Cloudflare’s privacy policy and GitHub’s privacy statement for their practices.

Children

Priv is not directed to children under 13. If you believe we have received a child’s personal information outside the local app, contact us using the link below.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as Priv changes. The effective date above will change when we publish an update. For privacy questions or requests, contact the developer through Crawford AI and Web. We cannot access or recover your local vault, passphrase, or recovery code for you.
